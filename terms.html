Effective: November 12, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

A PERSONAL RECORD, NOT MEDICAL ADVICE Menopause Tracker AI logs and organizes what you enter. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose any condition, and it never recommends a treatment, a hormone product, or a dose. The AI pattern summary describes your recorded numbers only — it does not advise, recommend, or interpret what a pattern means. Decisions about hormone therapy and any other treatment belong to you and your clinician.

WARNING SIGNS Some symptoms need prompt medical attention — for example bleeding after your periods have stopped, palpitations with chest pain or breathlessness, or very low mood. When you log a possible warning sign, the app will always tell you to contact your provider promptly. In an emergency, call your local emergency number; in the US you can call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES Enter doses and schedules exactly as they appear on your own prescriptions. Reminders are a memory aid only — always follow the instructions from your clinician and pharmacist.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS Your record is stored on your device and can be exported in full at any time, free. The optional free account also keeps a synced backup, so a new phone can pick the record up.

MENOPAUSE PLUS (SUBSCRIPTION) Plus is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID (monthly or yearly, at the price shown on the purchase screen). It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store account settings. Plus adds the AI pattern summary and the before/after regimen-change comparisons. Logging, history, adherence, the doctor report, Apple Health sleep import, and all exports are free and never require Plus.
