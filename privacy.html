Menopause Tracker AI — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

Menopause Tracker AI is built to be private by design.

WHERE YOUR RECORD LIVES Everything you log — symptoms, cycle marks, hormone-therapy products and doses, other medications, adherence marks, notes, and reminders — is stored on this device, in the app's own storage. The app works completely with no account, and we cannot see your record.

OPTIONAL ACCOUNT (BACKUP & SYNC) If you choose to create the free account in Settings, the same record is also stored in our database (provided by Supabase) in a row tied to your account, protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. It is used for exactly two things: backing your record up, and syncing it to a new phone when you sign in there. That is the only copy that ever leaves your device, and only while you are signed in. Signing out stops syncing; "Delete account" in Settings removes the stored backup. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no sale or sharing of your data — there is nothing to sell, and we do not do it.

PURCHASES (MENOPAUSE PLUS) If you subscribe to Menopause Plus, the purchase is processed by your app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information only — never symptoms, doses, adherence marks, notes, or any other part of your health record.

AI PATTERN SUMMARY (PLUS) If you ask for an AI pattern summary, the app first computes averages and totals from your log on this phone, and sends ONLY those numbers (per-symptom averages, regimen product names and change dates as you recorded them, adherence counts, cycle totals) to our server, which uses Google's Gemini AI to turn them into a plain-language summary. Your daily entries, your notes, your name, and your email are never sent. If your log for the period contains a possible warning sign, no summary is generated at all — the app shows you the warning guidance instead.

APPLE HEALTH (OPTIONAL) If you turn it on in Settings, the app reads your sleep from Apple Health so nights you did not rate yourself can still show their hours in your record. The import is read-only — the app never writes to Apple Health — and the imported hours become part of your record (and of your account backup, if you use one). Your own sleep ratings always take priority over imported hours.

NOTIFICATIONS The app only sends notifications you create yourself — a medication or patch-change reminder you set. There are no promotional or "we miss you" notifications.

DELETING YOUR RECORD Settings → "Delete all my data" erases the entire record from this device immediately and permanently. Removing the app also removes its data. The account backup is deleted separately, under Settings → Account → "Delete account".
